ABOUT

Brkzyy (Blake Brooks) is a 23-year-old gamer, streamer and designer from Melbourne, Victoria. He creates content across Call of Duty, GTA and Roblox.

Former member of Lucky7 (BO2 clan channel — 200K+ subscribers).

His achievements include Fortnite Cash Cups during the 2020–2022 peak, a Duos Champion title in Fortnite Oceania servers, Top 250 in Call of Duty Ranked, and #2 placement in Liberty Falls Zombies (Black Ops 6).

In 2026 he transitioned into Roblox content creation and rapidly grew across multiple platforms with millions of views.
